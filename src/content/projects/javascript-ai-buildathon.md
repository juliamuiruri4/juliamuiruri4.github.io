This content series focuses on building intelligent experiences using the JavaScript stack and the latest AI tools and frameworks. It is designed for JavaScript developers who want to explore how to integrate AI into their applications, with a focus on practical, hands-on learning.

## Why it matters

AI application experiences are often presented as magic. I am more interested in making them accessible and understandable to developers, poping the hood to show how they work and how to build and optimize them.
